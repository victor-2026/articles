# Kristel Kruustuk — The CEOs building the future of AI are saying testing matters

**Full text:**

The CEOs building the future of AI are saying testing matters.

In May 2025, Chamath Palihapitiya was blunt: the industry used to dump its worst engineers into QA. In AI, he said, that job becomes the most important one in the building.

Now, more than a year later, Anthropic's CEO published an essay saying the industry needs to slow down and his first concrete ask wasn't a pause, it was independent testers checking the work before release.

Days after that, Microsoft's CEO stood on stage and said he loves the idea of third-party testers and we should take all the time we want to test things.

One big question I have: if the people building the foundation think they need more testing, then how many companies are shipping AI features on top of these models with none at all?

We can wait for the frontier labs to set the standard. Or we can set the pace ourselves.
