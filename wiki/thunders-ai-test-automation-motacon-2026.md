## What is Thunders

AI platform that **writes, executes, and self-heals tests from plain text**. Target: non-technical participants (no-code test automation).

## Key details
- Writes tests from natural language descriptions
- Executes tests automatically
- Self-heals test scripts when UI changes
- Presenting at MoTaCon 2026 (Ministry of Testing conference, Netherlands)
- Platform: AI-driven, text-to-test
