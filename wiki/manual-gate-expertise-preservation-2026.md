# Manual Gate: Preserving Expertise in AI-Augmented Work (Mitchell/IEEE, Sep 2026)

**Sources:** [IEEE Spectrum](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-engineer-skills) (Richard Mitchell, 02.09.2026, full text fetched) via [Richard Self post](https://www.linkedin.com/) 12.09 · [Bainbridge 1983](https://web.archive.org/web/20200913080929/https://www.ise.ncsu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Bainbridge_1983_Automatica.pdf) (via Bolton comment)

## Data (career ladder breaking)

- Harvard working paper (65M workers, 280K firms): junior employment −9% within 6 quarters after GenAI adoption; seniors keep growing. Stanford ADP: youngest in AI-exposed occupations lost ground post-2022; losses concentrate where AI *automates* (augment → juniors hold/rise). Counter-theory (NY Fed): remote work broke mentorship, not AI — same broken mechanism either way: **the apprenticeship channel**.
- *"You cannot become a senior engineer without first being a junior one. Expertise is not downloaded."*

## Pattern: deliberate manual gate

Workflow point where a human takes controls **not for speed, not only as interlock, but to exercise a decaying skill. Chosen by design.** Software-team example: junior reproduces + root-causes + writes capturing test **with AI OFF**, then model proposes fix; **disagreement between diagnoses = the design working** (surfaced before the bad day, not during).

## Precedents

- **Nuclear:** manual steps left inside auto-executable sequences — *"inefficient by design, on purpose."*
- **Aviation:** AF447 (competence failure, not hardware); FAA SAFO 17007 (2017) — manual flight declared foundation; airlines trade fuel efficiency for hand-flying practice.
- **Bainbridge 1983** (Ironies of Automation): supervisor-only operators stop being operators; automation quits exactly on the worst day.

## Why it matters for us

1. **Human gate, upgraded.** Our gate was framed as safety interlock ( mui25/26). Manual-gate adds the second reason: **expertise preservation**. A gate that only rubber-stamps still lets skills decay (bored-observer failure: safety drivers, radiographers). Confirm/dismiss actions must require *judgment*, not clicks — design criterion for QAEverest-style review UX.
2. **Disagreement-as-signal.** Junior diagnosis vs model diagnosis compared explicitly — same epistemics as mutation matrix (oracle vs oracle) and DoorDash disprove-it. Candidate shared principle: never single-oracle.
3. **Junior pipeline vs 27.** Guided QA thesis (role promoted) needs the counterweight: who trains juniors when AI spares them the failures? Manual gate = the answer, quotable in 27 body.
4. **"Inefficient by design"** as budget language: deliberate inefficiency = insurance, bought on purpose. Arms the cost conversation (Antoine carrying-cost + Spotify token economics): some spend protects capability, not output.
