Product architecture can evolve faster than the quality operating model supporting it.

This is where Conway's Law becomes a practical QA leadership problem: quality ownership needs to follow stable capabilities, while accountability must remain tied to end-to-end business outcomes.

The most important shift is not adding another central release gate. It is making ownership, contracts, evidence and escalation explicit at the boundaries where business risk lives.
