The Inverse Conway Maneuver

Design the team boundaries you need to support the architecture you want.

Architecture as an organizational design choice

Shift one:
Quality ownership follows the capability.

  • functional correctness
  • API and event contracts
  • compatibility
  • regression safety
  • operational readiness
Capability teams own what they build

Shift two:
Accountability follows the business outcome.

A successful order is more than a successful checkout response.
  • created once
  • priced correctly
  • accepted downstream
  • recoverable after failure
  • traceable end to end
One named accountable owner

Shift three:
Central QA becomes enablement and governance.

  • standards
  • test platforms
  • specialist expertise
  • evidence models
  • risk governance
  • independent assessment
Not the final gate - the system of quality

At every important boundary, ask:

Who fixes it?
Who proves it?
Who is alerted when it fails?
If there is no answer, the boundary is not operationally owned.
