# Where Seeded Evidence Lives: At the Gate, Not in the Lane

Seeded evidence doesn't live in the testing lane. It lives at the governance gate every outsider lifecycle already has — release boards, procurement evidence requirements, regulated validation packages. Same slot, different names. This note answers the question outsiders keep asking: where does any of this live in *our* lifecycle?

Testing produces claims. Green suites among them. The gate is where someone outside testing asks why this particular green should be trusted — and that question exists everywhere, under whatever local name. A seeded verdict plugs into that slot as a fact (defects planted, caught or not) rather than another claim. Facts travel across governance, commerce, enterprise, and regulated worlds without translation. Claims need a passport; facts don't.

Our campaign lifecycle — seed, inject, run, classify, attest, close — answers how the evidence is produced. That was always our question. Gate placement answers where the evidence is consumed, and that was always theirs. The commercial hole was the missing second half: a method with a production line but no delivery address. Now it has one.

Four worlds, one slot. Governance calls it a release board. Commerce calls it procurement evidence. Enterprise calls it quality gates. Regulated calls it a validation package. Different names, same hunger: something that is true whether or not anyone believes the vendor. A planted defect, caught or missed, is exactly that kind of true.

So place it there. Not in the testing lane, where it competes with every other check for attention — at the gate, where someone is already waiting to ask the question it answers. The gate exists everywhere; only its name changes.

Don't copy our lifecycle — one pipeline is depth, not breadth. Copy the placement instead: produce evidence where you work, consume it where decisions happen, and never confuse the two addresses. Same rule as everywhere in this series: machines count, humans decide.

**Where does seeded evidence live in your lifecycle — testing lane, governance gate, or nowhere yet?**
