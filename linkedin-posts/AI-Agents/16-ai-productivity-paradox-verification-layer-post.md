Developers often feel faster with AI. The measured delivery result can be very different.

In a METR experiment, engineers expected AI assistance to make them roughly 20% faster. Measured task completion was 19% slower.

This does not mean AI "does not work." It means productivity moved to work we still measure poorly: supervising agents, checking plausible output, diagnosing drift and proving a change is safe to ship.

The bottleneck is no longer only code generation. It is verification.

I collected the available data, a practical maturity model and four Quality Engineering responses in the article linked below.

**Generating code became dramatically cheaper. Verifying it did not.**
